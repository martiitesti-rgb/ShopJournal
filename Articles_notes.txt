-Hybrid recommendation by incorporating the sentiment of product reviews:

Here it is analysed how recommender systems are used in the e-commerce world and how fundamental they are and how sentimental analysis can be applied in this particular case. In particular section 2.3 highlights sentiment analysis can be incorporated into those systems and how it can be used as a way of extracting relevant information from an item description. 
The paper mainly talks about some experiments conducted using BERT which is a “new method of pre-training language representations” developed by Google. I think the testing part especially is an interesting example on how to conduct test on the data taken from the datasets.


- Sentiment Analysis & Affect Scoring Pipeline:
  __VADER: This paper describes the development of VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary for
	sEntiment Reasoning) a lexicon that has been used in social media especially to classify 	texts as well as humans would,  analysing people's opinions, sentiments, evaluations, 		attitudes,
	and emotions via the computational treatment of subjectivity in text. It was useful to 		understand how words are scored and classified based on their meaning and how the 		lexicon was trained to understand the context in which words were used.
	VADER will be helpful for quickly processing review texts without requiring heavy model 	training.
